How to grind properly

Everything the dashboard does, how XP and badges are scored, and how to fix things when Discord or Steam act up.

01Getting started

  1. Run the app. Double-click GrindTracker.exe (download it from the Releases page ↗), and it opens in its own window. Running from source? Use npm install once, then npm start.
  2. Connect your accounts. The first time, a Connect your accounts window pops up. Paste your Discord Application ID, your Steam Web API key, and your Steam profile link. Each step links to the website you need.
  3. Keep the Discord desktop app open. Rich Presence only works with the desktop app, not Discord in a browser.
  4. Leave Grind Tracker running while you study and game. In the desktop app, closing the window quits it. Turn on ⚙ Settings → Keep running when I close the window to keep your Discord card going, and Start with Windows so it's always running.

Just looking around? Pick Use demo data for now. The dashboard fills with sample Steam stats. You can connect later from the Discord chip at the top right.

02The study timer

  • Press Start studying or Space. Press again to end the session. The timer keeps running even if you close the tab or restart the app.
  • The browser tab title shows the live timer (● 00:42:10 · Math), so you can check it from any tab.
  • Forgot to start it? Type minutes into Forgot the timer? and press Add. Added time counts toward your goal, XP and subject totals.
  • After 3 hours nonstop, the dashboard asks Still studying?, then again every 2 hours. That way a timer you forgot about can't inflate your stats.
  • The hero's segmented bar fills toward your daily goal. You get confetti when you hit it. Change the goal and your game budget under ⚙ Settings.

03Subjects

  • Pick a subject from the chips above the timer, from any row in the 04 · Subjects card, or with keys 1–9. Click the same subject again to clear it.
  • Switching subjects mid-session splits the session at that moment, so each subject gets exactly the time you spent on it.
  • Add your own subjects (up to 12) at the bottom of the Subjects card. Removing a subject hides it from the picker, but its past time stays in your history.
  • Your current subject shows on Discord, e.g. locked in · math.

04XP & ranks

XP is calculated from your whole study history every time the dashboard refreshes, so nothing can get out of sync:

2 XPper minute studied
+
100 XPbonus for each day you hit your goal

There are 50 levels: 10 ranks with 5 divisions each (I → V), from Rookie I to Legend V. Each level needs a bit more XP than the last (level L takes 25 × (L − 1) × L XP in total), so the first levels come in minutes and Legend V is the long game. Quests add +50 XP each, plus a bonus for clearing all three.

When you level up, you get a full-screen LEVEL UP moment. Your level also shows on your Discord card (· lv 7).

05Badges

There are 18 badges in a few categories. Like XP, they're scored from your whole history, so the time you studied before a badge existed still counts.

06Steam stats

  • Played today: Steam only reports lifetime playtime, so Grind Tracker records each game's total the first time it runs each day and counts the difference. The game you're in right now is counted live.
  • Achievements covers the game you're playing (or played last): completion, your rarest unlock (with the % of all Steam players who have it), your latest unlocks, and the easiest ones to get next.
  • Rarity tiers: < 5% ultra rare < 15% rare < 40% uncommon common
  • Your Steam profile's Game details must be Public, or Steam won't share any of this.

08Log & export

  • 06 · Today's log lists every session and added block of time today, newest first.
  • Made a mistake, like a timer left running? Click × and then Delete? to remove that entry. Your totals, XP and badges update right away.
  • Added too much? Type the minutes under the timer and press − Remove. It takes the newest time off first. You can also click any duration in the log and type the exact amount. Timed sessions can only be shortened.
  • Export CSV downloads your full history (date, start, end, minutes, subject) to open in Excel or Google Sheets.

10Troubleshooting

Nothing shows on my Discord profile

Check that the top-right chip on the dashboard says Discord live. If it says "Waiting for Discord", open the Discord desktop app. In Discord, go to User Settings → Activity Privacy and turn on Share your detected activities with others. Your status also can't be Invisible.

It says demo mode

Click the demo-mode banner (or press C) and finish the three connection steps. They save instantly, with no restart needed.

"Played today" is 0 or wrong

Set Steam Game details to Public, and start Grind Tracker before you play, since it counts from its first run each day.

"Steam rejected the API key"

Copy the key again from steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey ↗. It's exactly 32 letters and numbers, with no spaces.

Port 3000 is taken by another program

The app tells you which program is using it and asks whether to close it. Choose No and it moves to the next free port (3001, 3002, … up to 3020); opening the exe again still finds it.

How do updates work?

The desktop app checks GitHub when it starts, every 30 minutes, and when you open the window. A new version downloads in the background (you'll see a progress bar), is checked against GitHub's published checksum, and shows a Restart now banner. If the window is closed it switches over by itself. Your data is backed up first and is never touched by updates. Turn this off, or check manually, in ⚙ Settings → Updates.

11Focus timer

  • Switch the Session card from Timer to Focus and press Start focus (or Space).
  • A round is 25 minutes of focus, then a 5-minute break; every 4th break is a 15-minute long one. Change the lengths right on the card with the + / − steppers (or type a number), or tap Classic / Deep / Flow / Sprint.
  • The ring drains as time runs out, a soft chime plays between phases, and your Discord card counts down (focus 2/4 · math).
  • It runs inside the app, so it keeps going with the window closed. Each finished round is saved as a study session, so it counts for your total, XP, subjects and quests. Skip jumps to the next phase, and Stop ends the cycle (a cut-short round still counts as study time).

12Daily quests

  • Every day you get 3 quests from different categories: study time, sessions, focus rounds, variety, habits and balance.
  • Each one is worth +50 XP, and clearing all 3 adds a +100 XP bonus. Clear all 3 on consecutive days to build a quest streak, and on 5 days for the Quest Master badge.
  • Quests are the same all day (even after a restart), and a finished quest stays finished. New ones arrive at midnight.

13Privacy & notifications

  • ⚙ Settings → Privacy lets you pick exactly what your Discord card shows: study time, play time, level, current subject, the game you're playing, the goal bar, the timer, the picture and the Steam button. A live preview shows the result. Show my activity on Discord hides the card completely, while tracking keeps working.
  • ⚙ Settings → Notifications: Windows notifications for your daily goal, going over your game budget, focus rounds and breaks, finished quests, a break reminder after long sessions, and an optional "no study yet today" nudge at a time you pick. Use Send test to see one.

14Themes & your art

  • ⚙ Settings → Appearance: Mode colors (the default) change with what you're doing. Sakura, Ocean, Ember, Matcha and Mono keep one look, and Custom lets you pick both colors yourself. The background animation and these Guide pages follow along.
  • Upload your own character art (a tall image works best) and logo (cropped to a circle from the center). After choosing a file, a cropper opens: drag to position it, scroll or use the slider to zoom, then Save. Reset brings back the defaults.
